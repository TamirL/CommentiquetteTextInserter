## Scoring rules (as agreed)

| What you captured | Points |
| --- | --- |
| Each **8** | 0 (no points) |

| **2 of clubs** | 2 |
| **10 of diamonds** | 3 |
| **Majority of cards** at the end of the game | 3 (independent of everything else; a 26–26 tie awards nobody) |

Fixed card points per game: 4 (jacks) + 4 (aces) + 2 (2♣) + 3 (10♦) = **13**, plus **3** for the card majority = **16**, plus 1 per Basra on top.

> Note: the 2♣ and the 10♦ are scored only by their special value — they're not a Jack or Ace, so there's no double counting.
